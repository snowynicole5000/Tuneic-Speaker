Bead by bead, left to right. Notched beads read vowel first.

    Bead chart

    Each bead is one syllable. Shape is the consonant, color is the vowel. A plain round bead has no consonant, a white bead has no vowel, a notch means the vowel comes first, and a knot ends each word.

    Sound chart and tips

    Solid chips are consonants (low notes). Dashed chips are vowels (an octave up). Every sound starts on 1. Tap a sound to hear it.

    Invented names are guessed from spelling. To spell sounds yourself, put them in curly braces: {AY UH OO V ORE EE UH}
